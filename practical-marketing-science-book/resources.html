This appendix collects the public resources the further-reading sections draw on, grouped by the part of the book they support. Within each group, links keep the order of the original library. Where a link’s own text was just its address, the entry shows the site and path instead. Links to private workspaces (internal Slack, Notion and app sessions) have been removed. Some links point to shared Google Drive or Colab files whose access depends on the owner’s sharing settings.
