Marketing Science & Measurement Expert

I help companies find out what their marketing actually does, and move budget to what works. I spent six years in marketing science at Facebook / Meta and TikTok, led analytics teams at Cheapflights (acquired by Kayak) and MindGeek, and since 2022 I have advised brands as an independent marketing scientist.

I specialise in Marketing Mix Modeling, Incrementality, Attribution and Causal Inference, and I wrote Practical Marketing Science, a free field guide to measuring what marketing actually does.
